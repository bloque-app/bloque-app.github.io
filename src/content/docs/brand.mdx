Bloque's open-source projects are one family. Every mark follows the same grammar: rounded square
blocks in the product's hue and one round stop, on a 5 × 5 grid from 32 px up and a 3 × 3 grid
below. Each silhouette says what the product does, so the marks stay distinct without colour. The
violet full stop closes every wordmark, with "by bloque" beneath or beside it.

| Product | The mark |
| --- | --- |
| **aledgent** | The ledger: the book, closed. |
| **bezent** | The bridge: a span over two piers. |
| **rutent** | The ramp: two lanes, in and out. |
| **evmhot** | Hot wallets: every address, one centre. |
| **next** | Reserved. Mint is held for the next product; it has no mark yet. |

The names are set in Familjen Grotesk 700, tracked at −0.045em. Bloque's own wordmark is plain
"bloque" in the same face; this site's icon is a "b" drawn on the family's grid, closed by the
violet stop.

## Colours

<Swatches />

Orange and mint are light on white (2.61 and 2.14 : 1), so they work as block masses and never as
text or thin details. On ink, aledgent's violet becomes lilac `#a78bfa` and evmhot's crimson is
lifted to `#e8475f`; the stop turns white. Violet and sky, and orange or crimson and mint, merge for
some colour-blind readers, which is why every silhouette differs: hue is only the second cue.

The neutral grounds are Bloque's: ink `#0d0c17`, paper `#fbfaff` and white. The "by" of "by bloque"
is `#6d6982` on light grounds and `#9a96ad` on ink.

## Clear space and minimum sizes

<ClearSpace />

- **Clear space**: keep one block of the grid clear around the symbol or the lockup, on every side.
  A block is a sixth of the symbol's height: 16 px around a 96 px symbol.
- **Symbol**: the 5 × 5 symbol at **32 px or more**. Below 32 px, use the 3 × 3 version
  (`favicon-16.svg`), never the 5 × 5 one scaled down. Nothing smaller than 16 px.
- **Lockup**: at least **40 px** tall on screen (about 15 mm in print), so that "by bloque" stays
  legible. Smaller than that, use the symbol alone.

## Do and don't

**Do**

- Use the files below as they are, on the ground they are made for: `lockup.svg` and `symbol.svg`
  on light grounds, `lockup-dark.svg` on dark ones.
- Use a one-colour version (ink or white) where the product's hue cannot be printed or would clash.
- Keep the clear space, and keep the marks above their minimum sizes.

**Don't**

- Recolour a mark, swap one product's hue for another's, or put a mark in a gradient.
- Stretch, rotate, outline, shadow or re-draw a mark, or move, add or remove a block.
- Set the wordmark in another typeface, or drop the stop or "by bloque" from a lockup.
- Put a full-colour mark on a busy photo or a ground of a similar hue.
- Use a mark for a fork or for a product that is not Bloque's (see
  [names and marks](../open-source/#names-and-marks)).
